================================================================================
CONFUSED
d20 SRD Treasure Generator
Unresolved ambiguities in the SRD, in order of importance to resolve, followed
by ambiguities the app has already resolved.
================================================================================

PART ONE — STILL UNRESOLVED
These are points where the SRD is silent, self-contradictory or under-specified
and the app has had to make a call that is not justified by the text. They are
listed in descending order of how much they affect the fidelity of a generated
hoard. Each carries the app's current behaviour so a DM can see what to change.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Which ability pairs are "incompatible" on a magic weapon
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Both special-ability tables say to reroll "an ability incompatible with an
ability that you've already rolled", and neither enumerates which pairs are
incompatible. The app derives the matrix from the ability descriptions, each of
which says it supersedes another:

    Flaming burst   functions as a flaming weapon
    Icy burst       functions as a frost weapon
    Shocking burst  functions as a shock weapon
    Vorpal          functions as a keen weapon (against creatures with no head)
    Throwing / Returning  both make the weapon return to the wielder

WHY IT MATTERS: this changes what weapons can be generated at all. A DM who
allows Flaming and Icy burst on the same sword will find this app rerolls the
second.
RESOLUTION NEEDED: an explicit list of incompatible pairs.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. How to enforce the weapon +10 limit, given the SRD's own ordering
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Table: Weapons says "Special ability and roll again", so the ability is rolled
BEFORE the enhancement bonus is known. Footnote 5 says to reroll an ability that
"puts you over the +10 limit", which can only be judged once the bonus is known.
The two rules cannot both be followed literally.

The app rolls the ability first (as the table directs), then, once the bonus is
known, discards the most recently rolled abilities until the total is within
+10, logging each discard.
RESOLUTION NEEDED: whether to discard abilities, or to reroll the whole weapon,
or to roll the enhancement bonus before the ability.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. Cursed items do not replace the rolled item
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The SRD describes each cursed item as a specific kind of thing with a specific
apparent form: a berserking sword is a sword, armor of rage is armour, a necklace
of strangulation is a necklace. The app rolls an ordinary item first, then rolls
a cursed item and overwrites it, keeping the ordinary item as the "apparent
form". Because the cursed list is not filtered by category, this can produce a
rolled wand re-labelled "cursed armor", or a rolled ring re-labelled "vacuous
grimoire".

WHY IT MATTERS: this is the most visible absurdity the app can produce.
RESOLUTION NEEDED: the SRD gives no rule for generating a cursed item's host.
The app needs to discard the rolled item and generate a plausible host of the
matching kind.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. Intelligent weapon alignment versus its alignment-oriented abilities
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The SRD says a random intelligent weapon's alignment "must fit with any
alignment-oriented special abilities it has". The app rolls alignment on the
standard intelligent-item table independently of the weapon's abilities.
RESOLUTION NEEDED: whether to roll alignment first and then force the ability to
match, or roll the ability first and constrain the alignment, or reroll.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. Whether the 5% cursed-item roll applies to potions, scrolls and rings
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Magic Item Basics states "Randomly generated items are cursed 5% of the time"
without limiting the category. But the cursed-item listings for potions and
scrolls describe dedicated cursed forms — potion of poison, potion of delusion,
cursed scroll — rather than a 5% rule, and a cursed scroll explicitly does not
activate when deciphered, which is a different mechanism from a generic curse.
The app applies the 5% roll uniformly to every magic item.
RESOLUTION NEEDED: which categories the 5% rule covers.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. No distribution exists for the special-material roll's second half
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The SRD says to roll d% for a weapon or armour's material: 01-95 standard,
96-100 special material. It gives no distribution among the special materials
themselves. The app rolls uniformly among the LEGAL options for that item kind.
RESOLUTION NEEDED: a weighting, or confirmation that uniform is intended.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. No distribution exists for a bane weapon's designated foe
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The bane ability says only "Designated foe". The app uses one uniform list across
all tiers. The slaying-arrow table, which DOES have a 31-band d% distribution and
is the same idea, is used only for slaying arrows and not for bane.
RESOLUTION NEEDED: confirmation that the slaying-arrow table may be reused for
bane, or an independent bane table.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. "Any other size" for randomly found armour and weapons
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Magic Item Basics: armour and weapons found at random are Small on 01-30, Medium
on 31-90, and "any other size" on 91-100. No distribution is given for the last
band, no list of eligible sizes, and no pricing guidance beyond the armour table.
The app resolves it 01-40 Tiny, 41-85 Large, 86-100 Huge — an invention.
RESOLUTION NEEDED: the distribution, the eligible sizes, and how weapon damage
and cost are derived for them.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. Weapon cost and weight for sizes other than Small, Medium and Large
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The SRD states only that a weapon costs the same for Small and Medium and twice
as much for Large, and that weight is halved for Small and doubled for Large.
Nothing is given for Tiny, Huge, Gargantuan or Colossal. The app extrapolates
the same progression.
RESOLUTION NEEDED: actual figures.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. Weapon damage for non-Medium sizes when the weapon is not one of the nine
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Table: Larger and Smaller Weapon Damage gives nine example weapons. It does not
say how to handle any other weapon. The app interpolates: an exact Medium-damage
match uses that row, otherwise the nearest example's row, preferring the smaller
on a tie.
RESOLUTION NEEDED: a rule for the weapons the table does not list.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
11. Which scroll table a spell-storing ring uses
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The ring of spell storing says to determine its stored spells "as a scroll", but
does not say which table, nor what to do for a ring of spell storing, major,
whose ten-level capacity exceeds any single scroll tier. The app rolls arcane
scroll spells at a level of its own choosing and ignores rolls that would exceed
the budget.
RESOLUTION NEEDED: the table, and the procedure for a ring whose capacity
exceeds one tier.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
12. The staff and rod tables have no minor column
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Both tables have only Medium and Major columns, and Table: Random Magic Item
Generation confirms neither category exists at minor tier. The app falls through
to the Medium column when a minor-tier roll selects a staff or rod. An equally
defensible reading is to reroll the item category instead.
RESOLUTION NEEDED: confirmation of the fall-through.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
13. Whether an intelligent item's special purpose replaces a greater power
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Footnote 6 on the capabilities table says the item "can have a special purpose
(and corresponding dedicated power) rather than a greater power, if
appropriate". "Rather than" implies replacement, but no rule is given for how
many powers the item then has. The app adds the dedicated power alongside the
rolled greater powers rather than removing one, because removing one would
contradict the capabilities table's own count.
RESOLUTION NEEDED: whether the dedicated power replaces a greater power.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
14. Which of an intelligent item's three mental scores gets the 10
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The capabilities table says "Two at 15, one at 10" without saying which score
gets the low value. The app rolls for it. An equally defensible reading is that
Intelligence is always the low score.
RESOLUTION NEEDED: which score is the 10.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
15. How many spells a multi-spell scroll may hold and at what level
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Table: Number of Spells on Scroll gives 1d3/1d4/1d6 spells, and Table: Scroll
Spell Levels gives ONE level for the whole scroll. Neither says whether the
level is rolled once per scroll (the app's reading) or once per spell.
RESOLUTION NEEDED: confirmation.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
16. How a magic item's intelligence price modifier combines with its other
    modifiers
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The intelligent-item table gives a base price modifier for intelligence. The SRD
does not say whether it is added before or after the modifiers for the item's
existing special abilities. The app simply sums everything.
RESOLUTION NEEDED: the order of operations.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
17. The SRD's scroll price assumptions are prose, not data
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The scroll tables carry five footnote assumptions (XP cost up to 2,000; no
material component over 1,000 gp; no XP cost over 300; creatures of 10 HD or
less; higher ceilings on the top-level tables). They are stated as prose and are
not attached to individual rows, so a scroll's price cannot be reproduced
exactly without encoding them. The app prints them as text.
RESOLUTION NEEDED: per-row component and XP costs, which the SRD does not give.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
18. Whether the epic treasure counts also roll the 20th-level row in full
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The SRD gives levels 21-30 as "+N major items" without saying whether the
20th-level row should also be rolled in full or only the major-item column
consulted. The app rolls the full 20th-level row and then adds the bonus items.
RESOLUTION NEEDED: confirmation.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
19. Whether families of wondrous items use the table price or the description
    price
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The wondrous-item tables list families (feather tokens, ioun stones, figurines
of wondrous power, bags of tricks, necklaces of fireballs) as single rows with
one price each, but the descriptions give each variant its own powers and in
some cases its own price. Where the two disagree, there is no rule for which
wins. The app uses the table price.
RESOLUTION NEEDED: which price wins.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
20. Whether a darkwood shield from the mundane table is the same item as the
    specific shield
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Table: Mundane Items routes darkwood to a buckler/shield subtable priced 205 gp
and 257 gp — the same figures as Darkwood buckler and Darkwood shield on the
specific-shields table. It is genuinely unclear whether these are two generation
paths to one item or two different items.
RESOLUTION NEEDED: a ruling.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
21. The purpose-table choices have no distribution
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The intelligent-item purpose table requires a race for "defeat/slay a particular
race" and a deity for "defeat/slay the servants of a specific deity". No list or
distribution is given for either. The app uses an invented list of eighteen
races and twelve generic deity descriptions, chosen uniformly.
RESOLUTION NEEDED: the lists, which are necessarily setting-specific.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
22. Table: Average Treasure Results has no stated use
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The SRD gives average values for gems (275 gp), art objects (1,100 gp), mundane
items (350 gp) and each magic tier (1,000 / 10,000 / 40,000 gp) but no
instruction on how to use them. They appear to be diagnostic only. The app does
not expose them.
RESOLUTION NEEDED: an intended use, if any.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
23. Whether "Roll again twice" cascades
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
If a "Roll again twice" result itself lands on "Roll again twice", it is not
clear whether the cascade continues. The app cascades to a depth of four and
then stops.
RESOLUTION NEEDED: a depth limit, or confirmation that it is unbounded.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
24. Whether rerolls are recorded
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The SRD's reroll instructions produce discarded results. It does not say whether
they should be recorded. The app records them for weapon abilities with the
reason for each discard, and does not record them for armour abilities.
RESOLUTION NEEDED: nothing in the SRD; a consistency decision for the app.

PART TWO — RESOLVED
These are points where the SRD was ambiguous, contradictory or incomplete and
the app has made and documented a call. Listed so a DM can review the decisions
rather than discover them. Each states the ambiguity and what the app now does.

A. The SRD's greater-power table overlaps itself
   The table prints "86-91 Item can locate creature 3/day" and "91-97 Item can
   use fear against foes 3/day". Both bands include 91, and the bands sum to 101
   rather than 100. This is a typesetting error.
   APP: transcribed as 86-90, so that 81-85, 86-90, 91-97 and 98-100 partition
   d% 81-100 exactly. The error is noted in the data file.

B. Assassin's dagger is listed at two different prices
   Table: Specific Weapons lists it at 10,302 gp; its own description reads
   "Price 18,302 gp". An internal SRD inconsistency.
   APP: uses the table figure (10,302 gp) and prints a note flagging the
   discrepancy on the generated item.

C. The SRD titles some spells "X, Lesser/Greater/Mass" while the item tables use
   the adjective-first form
   For example "Restoration, Lesser" on the spell page and "Lesser restoration"
   on the potion table.
   APP: a 110-entry alias map plus a normaliser that also handles the SRD's
   typographic apostrophes, so both forms resolve to the same spell.

D. The SRD publishes a bane designated-foe distribution after all
   The 31-band d% table is printed inside the Bane ability description, identical
   to the slaying-arrow table. An earlier version of this log wrongly stated
   that no distribution existed.
   APP: transcribed once and used for both bane weapons and slaying arrows.

E. Table: Larger and Smaller Weapon Damage is not a single ladder
   The nine example weapons do not share one uniform progression. Deriving the
   implied steps produces five contradictions: 1d6 goes to 1d8 in four rows but
   to 2d4 in the falchion row, and 1d10 goes to 2d6 for the bastard sword but
   1d12 for the greataxe.
   APP: stores the table itself and interpolates, using an exact Medium-damage
   match where one exists and otherwise the nearest example, preferring the
   smaller on a tie. Verified against 14 lookups reproducing the SRD's exact
   figures.

F. Armour versus shield is decided by the enhancement table, not a separate roll
   Earlier versions rolled an 80/20 armour-versus-shield split. The SRD's own
   Table: Armor and Shields already decides it, with separate "+1 shield" and
   "+1 armour" rows.
   APP: the invented roll is deleted and the SRD table's own rows are used.

G. Magic armour and shield base items are weighted, not uniform
   Table: Random Armor Type and Table: Random Shield Type are weighted d%
   tables with masterwork-inclusive costs.
   APP: rolls on them directly.

H. Magic weapon base items are weighted, not uniform
   Table: Common Melee Weapons, Table: Uncommon Weapons and Table: Common Ranged
   Weapons are weighted d% tables, and the ranged table has its own ammunition
   sub-table for the 01-10 "Ammunition (roll again)" result.
   APP: rolls on all of them, including the ammunition sub-table and every
   composite-bow Strength variant.

I. The ranged weapon ability table is separate from the melee one
   The SRD publishes Table: Ranged Weapon Special Abilities with three abilities
   the melee table lacks (Distance, Returning, Seeking) and without eleven
   melee-only abilities.
   APP: rolls ranged weapons on the ranged table.

J. Weapon special-ability reroll rules
   The four footnotes under the two tables — Keen piercing/slashing only,
   Disruption bludgeoning only, Vorpal slashing only, and the general
   duplicate/incompatibility/+10 rule — are implemented, with damage types taken
   from Table: Weapons so that a "B or P" weapon correctly passes a piercing test
   and a bludgeoning test but never a slashing test.
   APP: every reroll is recorded in the item's log with the reason for the
   discard.

K. Scroll spell levels apply to the whole scroll
   Table: Scroll Spell Levels sets one level for the scroll. An earlier version
   rolled a level per spell.
   APP: one level and one caster level for every spell on the scroll.

L. Scroll prices are per spell and vary within a level
   The SRD prints a market price against each spell. At 9th divine, most spells
   are 3,825 gp but gate is 8,825 gp and miracle 28,825 gp. A level x CL x 25
   formula cannot express this.
   APP: uses the printed per-spell figures and sums them for multi-spell
   scrolls, and uses 12 gp 5 sp for 0th-level scrolls rather than 12.5 gp.

M. Potion and oil prices are per row, not per formula
   Table: Potions and Oils carries its own price on every row, and the same spell
   at different bonuses has different prices — shield of faith +2 is 50 gp but
   +3 is 300 gp; barkskin +2 is 300 gp but +3 is 600 gp.
   APP: uses the printed price and no longer computes anything.

N. Potion versus oil is tracked per row
   The SRD marks eight entries as oils. The app labels them "Oil of ..." rather
   than "Potion of ...".

O. The staff table has no minor column and 21 staffs, not 22
   The app falls through to Medium for a minor-tier roll, and the staff list was
   rebuilt from the live page with corrected prices for sixteen staffs, the
   seven 65,000 gp school staffs, and each staff's exact spell list with its
   charge costs.

P. Staffs cannot be intelligent
   The staff Special Qualities table has no intelligent result — 01-30 means a
   clue to function, 31-100 means none. This differs from the ring, rod and
   wondrous-item tables, where 01 means intelligent.
   APP: uses the correct bands per item type.

Q. Charges on rods are not universal
   The SRD opens by saying rods "do not usually have charges". The app was
   giving d%/2 charges to every rod.
   APP: only the rod of absorption (its own two-roll procedure) and the rod of
   flame extinguishing (exactly 10, renewed daily) have charges.

R. Luck blade is four separate table rows, not a wish roll
   Table: Specific Weapons lists Luck blade (0 wishes) 22,060 gp, (1 wish)
   62,360 gp, (2 wishes) 102,660 gp and (3 wishes) 142,960 gp as distinct rows.
   APP: rolls on those rows rather than rolling a wish count.

S. Wand and staff charges both use d%/2
   The SRD says a staff has 50 charges when created, and Magic Item Basics gives
   the d%/2 rule for charged items found as treasure.
   APP: applies d%/2 (minimum 1) to both.

T. Curses are not removed by dispel magic
   The app states on every cursed item that remove curse, break enchantment,
   wish or miracle are required, and lists which cursed items cannot be
   discarded until the curse is broken.

U. Magic armour and shield caster level
   The SRD states that for an item with only an enhancement bonus the caster
   level is three times the enhancement bonus, and that where it has both, the
   higher requirement applies.
   APP: computes and prints it.

V. Shield hardness and hit points scale with enhancement
   Each +1 of enhancement bonus adds 2 to a shield's hardness and +10 to its hit
   points. The app states this on every shield.

W. Special materials have exact SRD costs and are legality-checked
   The app now uses the figures from specialMaterials.htm (adamantine
   ammunition +60 gp, light armour +5,000, medium +10,000, heavy +15,000, weapon
   +3,000, shield +2,000; mithral light +1,000, medium +4,000, heavy +9,000,
   shield +1,000, other +500/lb; alchemical silver +2/+20/+90/+180; cold iron
   twice cost plus 2,000 gp of enhancements; dragonhide double masterwork cost;
   darkwood +10 gp per pound of original weight), and rolls only among the
   materials legal for that item kind.

X. 608 spells transcribed in full
   Every spell in the SRD is now in the app with its school, complete stat block
   and complete description, generated directly from the individual spell pages.
   588 distinct spell names referenced by the item tables resolve to a verbatim
   description; the remainder are covered by the alias map. Zero placeholders
   remain in generated output.
