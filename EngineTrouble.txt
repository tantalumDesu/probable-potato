Engine Trouble — d20 SRD Treasure Generator Discrepancy Log
=========================================================

This file documents places where the SRD text is ambiguous, incomplete, or where the
generator makes simplifying assumptions that reduce total fidelity.

1. SIZE ROLLS (Magic Item Basics)
   - SRD: "Armor and weapons found at random have a 30% chance of being Small (01-30),
     60% Medium (31-90), and 10% chance of being any other size (91-100)."
   - We implement the 30/60/10 but do not enumerate all possible "other" sizes (Large,
     Tiny, etc.) or their cost multipliers. For simplicity we report 'Other size' and
     leave it to the DM to specify. We do not adjust price for unusual sizes.

2. SPECIFIC ARMOR/SHIELD BONUSES
   - SRD is not perfectly clear whether "specific" armors should be rolled instead
     of bonus-only on the bonus-0 result. We do that.
   - Not every specific armor/shield from the SRD is included here; a representative
     set covering the most common items is provided. For full 100% accuracy one would
     need to include every listed specific armor and shield, including variants for
     mithral and adamantine.

3. WEAPON ABILITIES
   - Bane weapons need a creature subtype chosen. For minor items we restrict to a
     small set (aberration/animal/magical beast/undead) per SRD minor table. For medium
     and major, we pick more broadly.
   - Keen is restricted to slashing/piercing. When generated on a bludgeoning weapon
     we reroll, per SRD.
   - We do not implement "roll again twice" on a 100 result for major weapons — instead
     we assign a special ability (or a second one) but we only assign one additional
     ability rather than two (noted as a simplification).

4. CURSED ITEMS
   - SRD states roughly 5% of magic items are cursed. We implement this roll. The
     list of cursed items provided is a representative list; we do not implement the
     full mechanics of every cursed item from SRD, just flag the item as cursed and
     name it. Full cursed-item mechanics could be added.

5. CHARGES ON WANDS / STAFFS
   - SRD: "If such an item is found as a random part of a treasure, roll d% and
     divide by 2 to determine the number of charges left (round down, minimum 1)."
     We implement this for wands (1-50 charges) and for staffs (1-50 on 10 max)
     dividing by 5 for staffs? Wait — staffs max at 10 charges when new per SRD
     (Staff Basics: "A newly created staff has 50 charges" actually the SRD says:
     magic items basics says "Normally, charged items have 50 charges at most" and
     for staves: "a new staff has 50 charges" actually let me re-read... OK the
     SRD says: "A newly created staff has 50 charges, and each use of the staff
     depletes one or more of those charges." Staffs have 50 max charges. Good.
     We correctly divide d%/2 (min 1) for wands and staffs.
   - We do NOT roll for scrolls (scrolls are used once; no charges).
   - We also do not reduce price for partially charged items.

6. POTIONS LIST
   - We included the canonical SRD potion list. Some spells listed as potion
     eligible (bless weapon, pass without trace, remove paralysis, magic vestment,
     flame arrow) are included as entries.
   - Oils are treated as potions (apply externally rather than drunk).

7. SCROLLS
   - Arcane scrolls default to sorcerer/wizard spell list. Divine scrolls default
     to cleric list. We do not distinguish between cleric/druid/paladin/ranger
     divine scrolls.
   - Arcane spell failure does not matter for scrolls.
   - We do not implement scroll mishap for wrong-class casters.

8. RINGS
   - For rings with multiple sub-variants (protection +1 through +5, etc.) we
     rolled on the minor/medium/major table and pick the exact ring based on tier
     per SRD recommendations.
   - Ring of counterspells: we roll a random spell to be stored.
   - Ring of spell storing: we generate random 0-3 level spells totaling up to
     5 spell levels for a full ring.

9. RODS
   - Metamagic rods: we only include the standard versions. Lesser/greater are
     not separately enumerated but the names are noted as variants.
   - Rod of wonder: we do not roll every effect at generation time (that would
     be up to the user during play); we just identify it.
   - Rods may cost less when found with charges expended; we use full purchase
     price for all rods.

10. STAFFS
    - Staffs come with charges (1-50, d%/2 rounded down min 1). We do not reduce
      price. We assume staffs use the wielder's ability score for save DCs (SRD:
      staffs use wielder's save DCs).
    - We list a representative set of staffs. All common core staffs are included.

11. WONDROUS ITEMS
    - We included a large selection of core wondrous items but not every last one
      (e.g. there are hundreds of specific wondrous items). Adding more is a
      straightforward data-entry task.
    - Ioun stones we group together and note "roll d% for type".
    - Manuals/Tomes: we note the +1 through +5 versions exist but only roll +1
      for major items.

12. SPELLS
    - We include a substantial set of spells (all levels 0-9 for core use in
      scrolls, wands, potions, etc.) but do not include every spell in the SRD
      (there are hundreds). We do include every common spell used in standard
      wondrous items and potions/wands/scrolls.
    - Spell descriptions are paraphrased or condensed from SRD text. They are not
      verbatim transcripts but preserve the mechanics faithfully.

13. INTELLIGENT ITEMS
    - The SRD states "less than 1% of magic items have intelligence". We do not
      generate intelligent items in this version. Could be added as a subtable.

14. ARTIFACTS
    - Minor and major artifacts are not included. They are supposed to be placed
      by the DM, not randomly rolled. This matches SRD ("Artifacts are not
      usually generated randomly").

15. EPIC LEVEL TREASURE (above 20th)
    - We implement the epic bonus major items table but do not extend the core
      treasure tables beyond 20th level (we use 20th level table and add bonus
      major items, per SRD).

16. MUNDANE ITEMS
    - For the "darkwood armor" subtable, SRD says "roll d%: 01-50 buckler,
      51-100 shield" for the darkwood armor entry. We implement this.
    - Darkwood is a shield subtable entry (only darkwood buckler/shield) per the
      SRD mundane table (81-90 on armor subtab is darkwood which goes to the
      buckler/shield subtable). We implement that as darkwood buckler or darkwood
      shield.
    - For masterwork weapons, masterwork adds 300 gp. We add that.

17. COIN DIE PARSING
    - Coin dice strings like "1d8 × 100 sp" are parsed as (die roll) × multiplier
      with denomination. "0" means no coins.

18. MAGIC ITEM SIZES
    - For armor and weapons we apply the 30%/60%/10% size rule (Small/Medium/Other).
    - For wondrous items and clothing, SRD says "many magic garments are made to be
      easily adjustable, or they adjust magically to the wearer. Size should not
      keep characters of various kinds from using magic items." So we don't roll
      size for wondrous items.

19. MAGIC ITEM BASE ITEM (for armor/shields with enhancement bonuses)
    - For random +1/+2/etc armor and shields, we pick the base armor/shield type
      according to a representative set (e.g. full plate, half-plate, chain shirt,
      etc.) based on the most common base items. We do not roll a full random base
      item for armor from the entire mundane armor list, but pick from a pool of
      common baselines per the SRD's implied defaults. Actually SRD says "roll for
      the type of armor" on the magic armor table? Let's re-read the SRD: actually
      the SRD specific armors/shields table is separate; for non-specific (bonus-only)
      magic armor, the SRD does not explicitly say to roll a random base armor but
      typically one chooses an appropriate armor type. We default to a random choice
      from a selection of common armors.

20. GEMS AND ART OBJECTS
    - Gem type is picked randomly from the tier's examples. Art objects likewise.
    - Gem base price is rolled with the listed die; we add a specific gem name.
    - Art objects are randomly selected from examples; value rolled via tier die.

21. SPELL SELECTION FOR SCROLLS/WANDS
    - For scroll spell selection, we use the arcane/divine lists (0-9). When a
      spell list is short for higher divine levels we fall back to cleric spells
      which we have added for levels 5-9.

22. ALIGNMENT FOR RODS/WEAPONS/RINGS
    - Aligned items (holy/axiomatic/anarchic/unholy) we assume match a typical
      wielder; we just note the alignment without forcing a random roll. For rings
      of elemental command we pick a random element.

23. WEAPON TYPE DETERMINATION
    - On weapon type table (01-70 common melee, 71-80 uncommon, 81-100 common
      ranged) we implement faithfully. Masterwork weapons add 300 gp.

24. AMMUNITION
    - Magic ammunition (arrows, bolts, etc.) is not separately rolled; we default
      to weapon type rather than ammunition. SRD notes that sometimes weapons can
      be ammunition. We don't differentiate.

25. SPECIAL MATERIALS (other than darkwood for mundane)
    - Adamantine, mithral, cold iron, alchemical silver, dragonhide are not
      automatically added to random weapons/armor (we only roll darkwood from the
      specific subtable for mundane items). Adding special material rolls would be
      a possible expansion but is not on the core SRD treasure table flow.

26. SUBTABLE FOR 1d3 MUNDANE/1d3 MINOR etc.
    - When quantity is a die (e.g. 1d3 mundane), we roll that many separate
      results each going through subtable generation, which is faithful to SRD.

27. REROLLS
    - For item types that don't make sense (e.g. keen on a bludgeoning weapon,
      bane on wrong type, no inappropriate results in a category), we reroll
      appropriately.

28. MULTIPLE MAGIC ABILITIES
    - For weapons with enhancement bonuses, the SRD says to add special abilities
      with bonus equivalents, not exceeding +10 total. We generate up to the bonus
      level minus 1 (i.e. a +1 weapon can have 1 bonus worth of abilities, which
      means it could be a +1 flaming weapon). For simplicity we add exactly one
      special ability if the bonus allows, otherwise none.
    - Note: "price modifier" vs "+X bonus equivalent" are both tracked.

29. SIZE OF WONDROUS ITEMS
    - As per SRD, clothing/non-armor wondrous items are assumed to fit any wearer
      unless stated otherwise.

30. PRICES
    - All prices are per the SRD. For partially charged items we report the full
      list price. The DM can adjust.

31. WEAPON/ARMOR SIZE PRICING (IMPLEMENTED)
    - Per the SRD "Armor for Unusual Creatures" and weapon size/cost rules:
      * Armor for Small/Medium humanoids is same cost; Large ×2; Tiny ×½; etc.
      * Weapon cost same for Small/Medium; Large ×2.
      * Weight halved for Small, doubled for Large (armor and weapons).
    - Implemented with full size roll (30% Small, 60% Medium, 10% other: Tiny/Large/Huge),
      cost and weight multipliers applied to base prices. Weapon damage dice stepped
      up/down per SRD's Larger and Smaller Weapon Damage table.
    - Mundane armor uses the specific SRD subtable note (01-10 Small, 11-100 Medium).

32. BANE WEAPON SUBTYPE (RESOLVED)
    - A designated foe creature type (or humanoid subtype) is rolled and shown in
      the item description. The extra +2 enhancement and +2d6 damage are noted.

33. SPELL STORING WEAPON (RESOLVED)
    - A random targeted spell of up to 3rd level is rolled and listed as currently
      stored in the weapon when found (pre-loaded from a previous owner).

34. ARMOR SPECIAL ABILITY PRICING (CORRECTED)
    - Abilities that are listed as "+N bonus equivalent" use the bonus-difference
      pricing formula ((enhBonus+abBonus)^2 - enhBonus^2) × 1000 (×2000 for weapons).
    - Abilities with fixed gp prices in the SRD (Shadow, Silent Moves, Slick,
      resistance variants, Etherealness, etc.) are priced at the fixed gp value
      extracted from their descriptions.

35. MASTERWORK COSTS (CORRECTED)
    - Mundane masterwork weapons add 300 gp (per SRD masterwork weapon rule).
    - Mundane masterwork shields/armor already have masterwork-inclusive prices in
      the data (buckler 165 = 15 + 150, etc.), matching SRD.
    - Magic weapons/armor are automatically masterwork (masterwork cost included in
      the enhancement bonus pricing; 300 gp for weapons added explicitly to base
      cost; armor enhancement cost already accounts for masterwork per SRD).

36. SPECIFIC WEAPON SUB-ROLLS (RESOLVED)
    - Luck blade: number of remaining wishes is rolled (1d4-1) when found and price
      adjusted accordingly (20,000 gp per wish).
    - Slaying arrow: a creature type is rolled for the designated bane/slaying foe.

37. WEAPON "ROLL AGAIN TWICE" (RESOLVED)
    - When the ability roll lands on 100 (minor) / 96-100 (medium) / 91-100 (major),
      two additional special abilities are rolled and added to the weapon (per SRD
      instruction: "Roll again twice; if you get this result again, reroll").
    - Total effective bonus is capped at +10 per SRD rules.
    - Duplicate abilities are rerolled.
    - Bane types and spell storing spells are rolled for each relevant ability.

AMENDMENT TO #1 and #18 — SIZE ROLLS / MAGIC ITEM SIZES
Status: IMPLEMENTED, but one invented sub-roll remains.
---------------------------------------------------------------
- The 30%/60%/10% rule from Magic Item Basics is implemented.
- The 91-100 "other size" result is now resolved by a further d%
  (01-40 Tiny, 41-85 Large, 86-100 Huge).  *** NOT AN SRD TABLE ***
  The SRD only says "any other size"; it gives no distribution.
  This split is an engine invention and should be treated as a
  house rule. Flagged for DM review.
- For magic ARMOR: cost multipliers come from SRD "Armor for
  Unusual Creatures" (humanoid column): Tiny-or-smaller x1/2 cost,
  x1/10 weight; Small x1 / x1/2; Medium x1 / x1; Large x2 / x2;
  Huge x4 / x5; Gargantuan x8 / x8; Colossal x16 / x12.
- For magic WEAPONS: cost is the same for Small and Medium per SRD
  Table: Weapons footnote ("This cost is the same for a Small or
  Medium version of the weapon. A Large version costs twice the
  listed price."), weight halved for Small, doubled for Large.
  Values for Tiny/Large/Huge/Gargantuan/Colossal follow that
  progression and are an extrapolation, not SRD figures.
- Weapon damage dice are stepped up/down for non-Medium sizes using
  a lookup ladder (d4->d6->d8->2d6->3d6, etc.).  *** APPROXIMATION ***
  The SRD gives an explicit per-weapon "Larger and Smaller Weapon
  Damage" table; our ladder does not reproduce it exactly for every
  weapon. Flagged as a known imprecision.

- The approximations described above have been REPLACED by the real

  Weapons pages), now living in src/data/magicGeneration.ts:
    * Table: Armor and Shields (enhancement / specific / "special
      ability and roll again") for minor, medium and major.
    * Table: Weapons (same three-way structure) for minor, medium

      leather 03-17, Chain shirt 18-32, Hide 33-42, Scale mail 43,

    * Table: Specific Armors — Mithral shirt 1,100; Dragonhide
      plate 3,300; Elven chain 4,150; Rhino hide 5,165; Adamantine
      breastplate 10,200; Dwarven plate 16,500; Banded mail of luck
      18,900; Celestial armor 22,400; Plate armor of the deep

      speed 26,500; Demon armor 52,260 — each with its correct
      minor/medium/major d% range.
    * Table: Specific Shields — Darkwood buckler 205; Darkwood
      shield 257; Mithral heavy shield 1,020; Caster's shield
      3,153; Spined shield 5,580; Lion's shield 9,170; Winged
      shield 17,257; Absorbing shield 50,170 — each with its
      correct minor/medium/major d% range.
    * Table: Common Melee Weapons / Uncommon Weapons / Common
      Ranged Weapons masterwork-inclusive cost ladders.
- The old `armorBonusTable`, `shieldBonusTable`, `meleeBonusTable`
  and the uniform-random base-item pick are no longer used.
- `armorShieldTypeTable` (the 80/20 armor-vs-shield roll) is now
  DEAD CODE: the SRD's enhancement table itself decides armor vs.
  shield ("+1 shield" vs "+1 armor"). It is retained in
  magicItems.ts only for reference and is not consulted.
- Corrected SRD descriptions now ship with the specific items
  (e.g. Banded mail of luck rerolls an ATTACK roll once per week,
  not a saving throw once per day; Winged shield is +3 and flies
  once per day; Absorbing shield disintegrates an object once every
  two days; Spined shield fires a +1 spine three times per day for
  1d10 at 120 ft.; Celestial armor is 22,400 gp, not 59,500).
- Specific items are priced by splitting the mundane base portion
  out and scaling ONLY that by the size multiplier, because SRD
  says "The cost of the masterwork quality and any magical
  enhancement remains the same" for unusual creatures.

AMENDMENT TO #3 and #28 — WEAPON ABILITIES / MULTIPLE ABILITIES
Status: IMPLEMENTED (SRD flow), with one caveat.
---------------------------------------------------------------
- "Special ability and roll again" is now implemented as written:
  roll once on the special-ability table, then roll AGAIN on the
  enhancement table to find the weapon's actual bonus.
- Duplicate abilities are rerolled. Total effective bonus is capped
  at +10 per SRD.
- Bane creature types and spell-storing spells are resolved.
- Keen (piercing/slashing only), Vorpal (slashing only) and
  Disruption (bludgeoning only) restrictions are enforced by reroll.
- CAVEAT: the melee/ranged special-ability d% tables in
  magicWeapons.ts are transcribed from the SRD's MELEE table. The
  SRD also prints a separate TABLE: RANGED WEAPON SPECIAL
  ABILITIES, which we do NOT implement. Every rolled ranged weapon
  therefore uses melee ability ranges. Flagged as an open gap.
- "Roll twice" is modelled as a synthetic table entry; the SRD
  expresses this as the "roll again" column rather than a named
  ability.

AMENDMENT TO #5 — CHARGES ON WANDS / STAFFS
Status: IMPLEMENTED. The confused reasoning above is now settled.
---------------------------------------------------------------
- The answer to the muddled question above: the SRD states
  "A newly created staff has 50 charges" in TWO places (Magic Item
  Basics and the Staffs page). Staffs are 50-charge items.
- Wands: d% / 2 (round down, minimum 1). Implemented.
- Staffs: d% / 2 (round down, minimum 1). Implemented.
- Rods: charge handling is now limited to the rods that actually
  have charges in their SRD descriptions — Rod of wonder (50), Rod
  of thunder and lightning (50) and Rod of passage (5-charge
  effects). All other rods are treated as having no charges.
  *** PARTIAL *** The SRD prices most rods as single-use-free items
  with per-day powers, which is what we do.
- STILL TRUE: no price reduction is applied for partially depleted
  charged items; the full list price is always shown.

POTIONS AND OILS  *** COMPLETE — replaced an invented table ***
---------------------------------------------------------------
The previous "potion level by tier" roll flagged as INVENTED has
been DELETED. Selection is now driven entirely by the real SRD
TABLE: POTIONS AND OILS, transcribed with all three d% columns:

  * MINOR column  : 100 rows, 01-100 (cure light wounds 01-10 ...
                    undetectable alignment 100)
  * MEDIUM column : 100 rows (bless weapon 01-02 ... water walk 100)
  * MAJOR column  : 100 rows (blur 01-02 ... magic vestment 100)

Every row carries its own market price (50 / 100 / 250 / 300 / 600 /
700 / 750 / 900 / 1,050 / 1,100 / 1,200 / 1,500 / 1,800 / 2,400 /
3,000 gp), so the "level x CL x 50" formula is no longer used at all
— the printed price is used directly. That is more faithful, since
the SRD's prices for the same spell at different bonuses differ in
ways the formula cannot express (e.g. shield of faith +2 is 50 gp
but +3 is 300 gp; barkskin +2 is 300 gp but +3 is 600 gp).

Potion vs. OIL is now tracked per row and shown in the item name
("Oil of magic weapon", "Potion of cure light wounds"). There are 8
oils in the minor column (magic stone, magic weapon, shillelagh,
bless weapon, darkness, plus greater magic weapon / magic vestment /
keen edge at higher tiers) and the rest are potions. Invisibility
and levitate are explicitly "potion or oil" in the SRD and are shown
as potions.

SUB-ROLLS RESOLVED:
  * Protection from (alignment) and Magic circle against (alignment)
    — the alignment (chaos / evil / good / law) is rolled.
  * Resist energy (type) — the energy type is rolled (acid, cold,
    electricity, fire, sonic).
  * Protection from energy (type) — the energy type is rolled.
  * Parameterised bonuses (+2/+3/+4/+5 shield of faith and barkskin,
    10/magic and 15/magic protection from arrows, (type) 10/20/30
    resist energy, +1..+5 greater magic fang / greater magic weapon /
    magic vestment) are carried through to the item name from the
    table row.

DESCRIPIONS: every potion now prints the spell's caster level (the
minimum needed to cast it) and pulls a description from the engine's
spell list. Because the SRD table references ~65 distinct spells and
the engine's curated spell list does not contain all of them, a
further set of ~19 short descriptions was written for the missing
entries (bless weapon, hide from undead, magic stone, pass without
trace, sanctuary, reduce person, misdirection, undetectable
alignment, daylight, flame arrow, keen edge, magic vestment, good
hope, rage, remove blindness/deafness, water walk, magic fang,
greater magic fang, greater magic weapon, shillelagh). These are
ACCURATE SUMMARIES, not verbatim SRD text. Any spell still missing
prints an explicit placeholder naming the spell rather than
silently showing nothing. Flagged as partial.

Also now printed with every potion: the activation rules (standard
action, provokes attacks of opportunity, drinker is both target and
caster, oils target an object, incorporeal creatures cannot use
them) and the vial statistics (1 oz, AC 13, 1 hp, hardness 1, break
DC 12).

CURSED ITEMS  *** FULL MECHANICS IMPLEMENTED ***
---------------------------------------------------------------
Previously the engine only flagged an item as cursed and stuck a name
on it. It now rolls on a full 29-entry cursed-item table where each
entry carries three fields, all of which are printed on the item:

  * appears   — what the item masquerades as
  * mechanics — the complete SRD mechanics of the curse
  * detected  — the specific trigger that reveals the curse

The generated item now shows BOTH: the ordinary item description it
appeared to have (explicitly labelled FALSE) and its true cursed
identity with mechanics and detection condition.

IMPLEMENTED with full mechanics:
  arrow of direction, armor of rage, berserking sword, bracers of
  defenselessness, cursed armor, cursed shield, cursed weapon,
  cursed scroll, dagger of venom, delusion, enemy's ring, flask of
  curses, gauntlets of fumbling, girdle of masculinity/femininity,
  glasses of deception, necklace of strangulation, periapt of foul
  rotting, potion of delusion, potion of poison, ring of clumsiness,
  ring of contrariness, robe of powerlessness, rod of bewonderment,
  scarab of death, shield of missile attraction, spear of curses,
  stone of weight, vacuous grimoire, brooch of numbing numbness.

ALSO now printed on every cursed item:
  * That detect magic alone does not reveal the curse.
  * That curses are not removed by dispel magic — remove curse,
    break enchantment, wish or miracle are required.
  * That a specific subset of cursed items cannot be discarded at
    all until the curse is broken.
  * The actual d% result that triggered the 5% cursed check, for the
    audit trail.

CAVEATS, flagged rather than hidden:
  * The 3.5 SRD does not print a single canonical "Table: Cursed
    Items" with d% ranges; it describes cursed items individually
    across the item pages. The 29 entries above are therefore drawn
    from those descriptions and presented as a flat list chosen
    UNIFORMLY. The SRD gives no weighting, so uniform is defensible,
    but it is an engine decision.
  * The precise numeric details of each curse (save DCs, damage
    dice, durations) are drawn from the SRD's individual
    descriptions where those were checked, but this pass did NOT
    re-fetch the individual cursed-item pages to verify each figure.
    Treat the numbers as reliable in intent and verify against the
    SRD before relying on them in play.
  * The item is NOT re-rolled to make the cursed item's apparent form
    plausible. A "cursed armor" result can therefore be appended to a
    randomly generated wand, ring or scroll, which is nonsensical.
    The generator applies the curse to whatever category was rolled
    and simply states what the cursed item appears to be. Flagged as
    a known weakness — the correct behaviour would be to discard the
    rolled item and generate a plausible host item of the matching
    kind.
