================================================================================
TURBOCHARGER
d20 SRD Treasure Generator
Changes that would make the app more complete, in order of priority.

Each entry states what is missing, why it matters, and roughly how large the
work is. "S" is a few hours, "M" is a day or two, "L" is a multi-day project.
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TIER 1 - CORRECTNESS. These produce wrong or absurd output today.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1.  Cursed items should replace the rolled item, not re-label it            [M]

    A rolled wand can currently be re-labelled "cursed armor", and a rolled ring
    re-labelled "vacuous grimoire". This is the single most visible absurdity the
    app produces and it appears in roughly one hoard in twenty.

    Fix: partition the 29-entry cursed-item list by the kind of item it
    masquerades as (weapon / armour / shield / ring / rod / wondrous / potion /
    scroll), roll a host item of the matching category, then apply the curse.
    Where the SRD states an apparent form explicitly - berserking sword is a
    sword, necklace of strangulation is a necklace - the host should be that
    item, not a random one.

2.  Intelligent weapon alignment must agree with its abilities              [S]

    The SRD says a random intelligent weapon's alignment "must fit with any
    alignment-oriented special abilities it has". The app rolls alignment
    independently, so it can produce a lawful good weapon carrying an unholy
    ability.

    Fix: after generating a weapon with holy, unholy, anarchic or axiomatic,
    constrain the intelligent-alignment roll to the alignments consistent with
    that ability. Holy permits good alignments, unholy permits evil, anarchic
    permits chaotic, axiomatic permits lawful. Where the constraint cannot be
    satisfied by the table, reroll the alignment.

3.  Double-weapon second-head pricing is rolled but not costed              [S]

    The second head's bonus is rolled (same bonus on 01-50, one less on 51-100)
    but the "one less" case does not reduce the enhancement cost the app reports.
    The special-abilities budget also treats the weapon as single-headed.

    Fix: compute both heads' bonuses, sum their squared costs, and exclude the
    second head from the special-ability roll as the SRD directs.

4.  Mithral weapon cost should be per pound                                [S]

    The SRD prices mithral at "+500 gp/lb" for items other than armour and
    shields. The app charges a flat +500 gp regardless of the weapon's weight. A
    mithral greatsword (8 lb) should be +4,000 gp, not +500.

    Fix: multiply the weapon's weight by 500.

5.  Ammunition quantities beyond the per-50 quote                          [S]

    The SRD's weapon base-price column is quoted per 50 arrows, bolts, shuriken
    or sling bullets. The app generates one pack and says so, but never rolls how
    many packs are in a hoard, and never notes the magic-ammunition breakage rule
    (50% chance of being useless on a miss, always destroyed on a hit).

    Fix: roll a number of packs, and print the breakage rule on magic ammunition.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TIER 2 - COMPLETENESS. Real gaps where the SRD has content the app lacks.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

6.  Nonstandard treasure adjustments                                       [M]

    The SRD's treasure chapter describes four adjustments the app does not
    implement at all: fractional coins (roll for coins but divide the result),
    "% goods or items" (roll d% first and only generate on a success), double
    goods or items (roll twice), and the parenthetical "no" / "only" notes that
    restrict what a creature collects.

    Fix: add a per-monster modifier panel alongside the CR selector, letting the
    user mark a hoard as fractional, double, or "% goods/items", and apply the
    notes. This is the feature that would make the app useful for preparing an
    actual encounter rather than a generic hoard.

7.  Treasure Values per Encounter and Average Treasure Results             [S]

    Both tables are in the SRD's treasure chapter and neither is exposed. They
    are the natural way to sanity-check a generated hoard: 300 gp at EL 1 up to
    80,000 gp at EL 20, and the expected value of each treasure type.

    Fix: show the expected value for the chosen level next to the generated
    hoard's actual total, with a "way over / about right / stingy" indicator.
    Cheap to build and immediately useful.

8.  Encounter-level generation                                             [M]

    The app generates by CR. The SRD's tables are keyed to encounter level, and
    for a mixed group of monsters the EL is not the sum of the CRs. There is no
    way to say "three CR 2 orcs and a CR 3 chief" and get one hoard.

    Fix: let the user enter a group of monsters, compute the encounter level per
    the SRD's rules, and generate one hoard for the encounter.

9.  Export                                                                 [M]

    A generated hoard cannot be saved, copied as text or downloaded. At the
    table this is the difference between a demo and a tool.

    Fix: a copy-to-clipboard button producing clean plain text, and a download
    producing Markdown and JSON. Also a session history so several hoards can be
    generated and compared.

10. Intelligent items: the SRD's demand and punishment lists               [S]

    The SRD lists eight demands a dominant item may make and six harsher
    measures it may resort to. They are not reproduced on the item, so the user
    has to look them up.

    Fix: include both lists in the generated item's text, since the item already
    reports its Ego and therefore whether the item can dominate.

11. The old curated spell list - DONE this pass                            [S]

    The complete 608-spell SRD set is in the app and the 194-spell curated list
    in spells.ts was no longer imported by the generator. It has been deleted.
    Verified: no file in src/ imports from data/spells, and the project builds
    cleanly without it.

12. Intelligent items on specific weapons of fixed alignment               [S]

    Related to Tier 1 item 2 but distinct: several specific weapons in the SRD
    are stated to be of a fixed alignment (sun blade is good, nine lives stealer
    is evil, holy avenger is good). An intelligent roll on such a weapon should
    adopt that alignment rather than rolling.

    Fix: detect an alignment statement in the specific weapon's description and
    pin the intelligent item's alignment to it.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TIER 3 - USABILITY. The data is right; the experience could be better.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

13. Per-item and per-hoard filtering and sorting                           [M]

    A CR 20 hoard can contain forty items. There is no way to sort by price, no
    way to filter to magic only, no way to collapse everything at once, and no
    way to hide the dice logs globally.

    Fix: sort controls on each section, a global "expand/collapse all", a "hide
    roll details" toggle, and a "magic items only" filter.

14. Searchable reference browser                                           [L]

    All 608 spells, 300 wondrous items, 34 specific weapons, 12 specific armours,
    8 specific shields, 41 rings, 35 rods, 22 staffs and every potion are in the
    app as data. None of them is browsable - the app only shows what it happens
    to roll.

    Fix: a second view that lists and searches the transcribed content. This
    turns the app from a generator into a reference as well, and it is nearly
    free because the data is already there.

15. Persistent session state                                               [S]

    A refresh loses the hoard, the CR and the seed. The seed field exists for
    reproducibility but is not preserved.

    Fix: persist the last hoard, CR and seed to local storage, and show the seed
    that produced a hoard so it can be recreated.

16. CR and seed in the URL                                                 [S]

    Two people cannot share a generated hoard by link.

    Fix: encode CR and seed in the query string and read it back on load.

17. Weight and load totals                                                [S]

    The app tracks no weight. A hoard's total weight is the practical limit on
    what a party can carry, and the SRD gives a weight for almost every item.

    Fix: store and total weight, and show it against the hoard total.

18. Dice log readability                                                  [S]

    A CR 20 hoard's log can run to hundreds of lines with no grouping. Rerolls
    are marked for weapon abilities but not for armour abilities.

    Fix: group the log by item, mark rerolls consistently, and allow a single
    roll to be expanded to see its raw die values.

19. Mobile layout                                                         [S]

    The CR selector and the wide item cards are cramped on a phone. Most use at
    the table is on a phone.

    Fix: a responsive pass on the CR selector and the item cards.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TIER 4 - BREADTH. Content the SRD has that the app does not touch.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

20. Artifacts                                                             [M]

    The SRD lists minor and major artifacts with full descriptions. The app
    generates none, on the reasonable ground that the DM places them - but there
    is no reason the app could not offer an optional "include artifacts" toggle
    for DMs who want one.

21. Monster treasure entries                                              [L]

    The SRD's monster entries state what treasure each creature carries and in
    what proportions. The app generates from CR alone and ignores this. Carrying
    the monster treasure table would let a user generate a hoard for a specific
    creature rather than a CR.

22. NPC gear and wealth by level                                          [M]

    The SRD gives wealth-per-level tables for player characters and NPC classes,
    which is how a replacement character's kit is usually generated. Not
    implemented.

23. Goods and services                                                    [M]

    The SRD's goodsAndServices page has the full armour, weapon, tool, animal,
    lodging, hireling and spellcasting-service price lists. The app has the
    mundane items table but not the rest. A shop generator would be a natural
    companion feature and would reuse most of the same data.

24. Random encounter generation                                           [L]

    The SRD has encounter tables by terrain and climate. Combined with item 8
    this would let the app generate a complete encounter including its treasure.

25. Cursed item mechanics for each individual curse                       [M]

    The app names a curse and describes it, but the numerical details of the 29
    curses were written from the SRD's individual descriptions as understood at
    the time and have not been re-verified against those pages. A pass to confirm
    each save DC, damage figure and duration against the SRD would close this.

26. Spell data: domains and levels per class                              [M]

    The 608 transcribed spells carry the Level line as the SRD prints it
    ("Sor/Wiz 2, Water 7"), which is faithful but not structured. Nothing in the
    app can ask "show me all 3rd-level cleric spells".

    Fix: parse the Level line into per-class levels, which would also let the
    divine scroll table pick from a specific class list rather than a merged
    divine list.

27. Spell descriptions for generated items are long                       [S]

    A scroll of nine spells now embeds nine complete SRD stat blocks and
    descriptions, which can run to several thousand words on one item card. The
    data is correct; the presentation is overwhelming.

    Fix: collapse each spell to its name by default with an expander for the
    full text, and add a "compact item cards" toggle.

28. Localisation of the item name prefix                                  [S]

    The app hard-codes English phrasing ("Oil of", "Potion of", "Intelligent",
    "CURSED"). Not a fidelity issue, but a barrier if the app is ever used in
    another language.

    Fix: move the prefixes into a single table so they can be translated.

================================================================================
IF ONLY THREE THINGS GET DONE
================================================================================

  1. Tier 1 item 1 - cursed items must replace the rolled item. It is the one
     change that stops the app producing visibly absurd output.

  2. Tier 1 item 2 - intelligent weapon alignment. Same reasoning: it produces
     combinations the SRD explicitly forbids.  (Item 11 is also done.)

  3. Tier 3 item 9 - export. Without it the app cannot be used at the table,
     which is the whole point of a treasure generator.

Tier 2 item 6 (nonstandard treasure adjustments) and item 7 (expected value)
would be the next two, because together they turn the app from a random hoard
generator into something that can prepare a real encounter and tell you whether
the result is reasonable.
